C. Bento grid

  • The most modern and the most "unique at a glance". Everything (work, stats, personality) is visible in one screen.
  • Live tiles (Duolingo, Now reading, map) make the site feel alive.
  • Busiest of the three. Needs strict design discipline or it turns chaotic.
  • Harder on mobile (tiles stack into a long list).
