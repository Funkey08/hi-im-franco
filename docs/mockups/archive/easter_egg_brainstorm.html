Professor Layton

Numbered hidden puzzles — umbrella mechanicStrongest unifying ideaFrame all your easter eggs as collectible numbered "Puzzles" (Layton's whole shtick) with a tiny unobtrusive counter somewhere ("Puzzles found: 3/8"). Konami-code Kurisu flash = Puzzle #1, scroll-to-bottom ASCII resolve = Puzzle #2, console.log art = Puzzle #3, GoL triforce = Puzzle #4, etc. This turns a pile of unrelated gimmicks into one coherent, intentional "hidden layer" of the site — which is itself a nice signal of structured thinking.
One real logic/math mini-puzzlemost build effort, most payoffSomewhere on the site, an actual small interactive puzzle (a riddle, a logic grid, a simple cipher) styled as "Puzzle #001", that unlocks a genuinely fun fact or the Kurisu reveal when solved. The one idea on this whole list that's actually interactive problem-solving, not just a visual trigger — strongest "I built something clever" signal, but also the most work.
